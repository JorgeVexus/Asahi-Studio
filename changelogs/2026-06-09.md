# Changelog - 2026-06-09

## Project: Asahi Studio - Premium Interactivity & Full SEO Overhaul

### 🚀 Overview
Major upgrade focused on two fronts: (1) making the site fully interactive on both desktop and mobile with premium animations, SVG art and micro-interactions, and (2) a complete technical SEO overhaul to drive organic traffic (structured data, sitemap, robots, indexable FAQ content, image optimization).

### ✨ Interactivity & Redesign
- **Mobile fullscreen menu (new)**: Animated circular clip-path reveal, staggered links with kanji accents, decorative hinomaru sun, dedicated top bar with brand + close button, body scroll-lock and Escape-to-close. Previously mobile had *no* navigation at all.
- **Smart navbar**: Hides on scroll-down past the hero, reappears on scroll-up; active-section highlighting via IntersectionObserver with animated underlines.
- **Hero SVG sun (redesigned)**: Replaced the flat circle with an animated SVG composition — 16 rotating sun rays, a brush-drawn enso ring (pathLength draw-on animation), kanji watermark, and the existing scroll parallax.
- **Animated stat counters**: Hero metrics (98 PageSpeed, 3.2x lift, 14d launch) count up on scroll into view.
- **Magnetic buttons (new `Magnetic` component)**: Cursor-attracted CTAs in Hero and Footer (desktop only, inert on touch).
- **FAQ section (new)**: Premium accordion with 9 questions — display typography, animated +/× toggles, sticky sidebar on desktop. Single source of truth shared with the ChatWidget (`faq-data.ts`).
- **Work section mobile**: Replaced the tiny 80px thumbnail with full-width 16:10 preview cards including tag bar and "Visitar" affordance. Desktop floating hover preview unchanged.
- **Premium footer (rebuilt)**: Seigaiha (青海波) SVG wave pattern divider, sitemap nav columns, services list, semantic `<address>`, magnetic CTA, giant animated ASAHI wordmark, back-to-top link, auto-updating year.
- **Touch & motion polish**: `whileTap` feedback on 3D service cards, marquee pauses on hover, tap-highlight removal, smooth scrolling with `scroll-margin-top` on all anchored sections, and full `prefers-reduced-motion` support (CSS + framer-motion `MotionConfig reducedMotion="user"`).

### 🔍 SEO Overhaul
- **index.html rewritten** as the static source of truth for crawlers: optimized title/description with target keywords, canonical URL, robots directives (`max-image-preview:large`), geo tags, full Open Graph set (og:url, og:locale es_MX, og:site_name, absolute image URLs + dimensions), complete Twitter Card, theme-color, and `lang="es-MX"`.
- **JSON-LD structured data (3 blocks)**: `ProfessionalService` (with the 3 package offers, price range, area served), `WebSite`, and `FAQPage` (9 Q&As — now legitimately backed by the visible FAQ section).
- **robots.txt (new)**: Allows all crawlers including GPTBot/ClaudeBot/PerplexityBot for AI search visibility; points to sitemap.
- **sitemap.xml (new)**: Canonical homepage entry at asahistudio.lat.
- **`__root.tsx` deduplicated**: Runtime head now only sets title + canonical; removed duplicate og/meta tags; fonts moved to index.html with preconnect for faster LCP.
- **Image optimization**: Converted 4 portfolio PNGs (4.1 MB) to WebP (348 KB, −92%); added `loading="lazy"`, `decoding="async"`, and descriptive Spanish alt text.
- **Semantic/a11y**: `aria-label`s on nav/sections/marquee, `aria-expanded` on toggles, `<address>` in footer, noscript fallback content with key business info.

- Verified live in preview at desktop (1280px) and mobile (375px): hero, mobile menu open/close/navigate, FAQ accordion, Work cards, footer.
